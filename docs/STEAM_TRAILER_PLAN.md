## Recording checklist

- [ ] Use the same verified build and capture profiles as the screenshot plan. Keep a log of source exports and actual gameplay actions.
- [ ] Resolve blocking decisions normally in the disposable copy. Do not inject rewards or bypass selection rules.
- [ ] Record UI interaction; avoid a slideshow of unmoving screenshots. Keep mouse motion deliberate and pause over the point being explained.
- [ ] Trim dead loading time for pacing without claiming a benchmark or “instant” long-save navigation. Do not disguise a mock-up as gameplay.
- [ ] Do not add unlicensed music/voice. Current game has no authored audio playback identified. A silent rough cut is preferable until trailer audio rights are confirmed.
- [ ] Check text readability on a small player, truthful sequencing and no private data. Retain source clips and edit project outside the shipped depot.
- [ ] End-card “Wishlist Now” is for publication once the store page is actually public. Until then this is an offline production plan; no fabricated store URL or release date.
