# Chalk restocking

Equipment > Chalk now separates buying a five-unit pack from equipping existing stock. The low-stock inbox action opens this shop, initially selecting the equipped brand. The detail card shows usable units, the pack price and a Buy another pack button for owned chalk. The equip button is free and separate.

Restocking adds five usable units and charges the catalogue pack price each time. It preserves usable chalk condition and the selected setup when buying stock for another owned brand. An exhausted current unit is replaced; it is not counted as usable stock. Insufficient funds and invalid item IDs cannot add stock or charge cash. Old saves without per-brand conditions are supported.

Checks cover stock addition, repeat purchase charges, retained wear, unchanged equipped brand, exhausted units, insufficient funds, and inbox-to-shop purchase/reload on desktop and mobile.
