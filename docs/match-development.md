# Match reviews, objectives, coaching and rivalries

## Where to find them

- Match Preview shows personal objectives and an expandable Tactical & scheduling advice panel. Use coach’s approach selects a real tactical plan; all selected preview tactics, mental focus and tempo now carry into live play.
- Live Match displays progress toward the locked objectives.
- Match Result shows recorded evidence, objective outcomes and a specific development recommendation. Start recommended project uses the existing protected training-project system; it never replaces an active project or rewrites the weekly timetable.
- Training and the calendar list expose scheduling guidance for the next eligible event.
- Rivalry disclosures in the Hub, Preview and Result show intensity, close matches, deciders, finals and the last eight meetings. Practice partners are labelled friendly rivals.

## Persistence and balance

Objectives use the actual round length, public opponent ranking and the player's break-building skill. They are frozen in the live session at kickoff and evaluated once at settlement. Single-frame events use a break objective, not a redundant win-a-frame goal; four-frame groups and long matches have bounded frame targets. Existing matches and already-running old sessions receive no retroactive objectives or rewards.

Completing all objectives adds at most one confidence point, including in defeat. Winning after the last recorded defeat to an established rival can add one more. These bonuses respect the confidence ceiling, are reported in the saved debrief, and cannot be replayed by reloading or settling twice. Career history tracks objective totals.

Debriefs preserve the available frame margins, highest breaks, break counts and prior recorded potting estimates. They recommend a named training project and concrete sessions. Simulated success rates are labelled as estimates, not proof of causality. Historical matches get a read-only review without invented targets.

Coaching recommendations use the player's condition and skills, actual previous meetings and the next eligible event. They do not reveal exact hidden opponent abilities. Advice protects imminent event preparation and recommends recovery when fatigued; it does not automatically skip tournaments or change bookings.

Rivalries develop through repeated close matches, deciding frames or finals. Single-frame events do not count as deciders. Existing rivalry history is retained. Opponents can counter repeatedly used tactics through the existing learned-counter system. New rivalries create a one-time inbox notice. Draws and eight recent meeting records persist across seasons.

## Checks

Unit coverage includes format-appropriate objectives, progress in defeat, missing historical goals, evidence-driven reviews, fatigue and schedule advice, rivalry persistence and tactical adaptation, duplicate protection, and confidence rewards with and without a rivalry. Browser coverage verifies preview-to-live tactical choices, live objective visibility, reload stability, post-match training actions and mobile layout, alongside the existing responsive training checks.
