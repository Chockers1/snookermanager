# Pending ranking credit

World and one-year rankings publish recorded event earnings on the scheduled event finish date. Playing all matches on an earlier in-game date does not bring publication forward. Exhibition income does not create world-ranking credit.

The Rankings page now lists positive, non-estimated recorded credits that are awaiting publication, with their event name, amount and exact date. The notice is omitted from other circuits and the final-ranking view; the one-year tab only includes its current season. Published entries disappear automatically. The display never awards money or modifies standings.

A regression verifies credit is excluded before publication, added once on publication, removed from the pending list and does not change cash. A read-only copy of the observed RT save confirms Wuhan's £56,000 credit is scheduled for 2027-08-29; the standings move from £644,997 at #21 to £700,997 at #20 with the currently recorded field results.
