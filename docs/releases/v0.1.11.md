This release improves match reviews and tournament navigation, makes skipping an invitation advance the career, and gives sponsors distinct reactions to results.

### Match reviews and draws
- Rebuild Match Review as a compact workspace with Overview, Statistics, Career Impact and Analysis tabs, plus group standings where applicable.
- Keep the result, next action and major-win presentation prominent. Open preparation, form evidence and rivalry details in dialogs.
- Add a fullscreen draw button to Tournament Hub and the full draw page, including group fixtures. Escape or Exit returns to the original view.
- Keep narrow-screen and enlarged-text layouts readable, with scrolling where needed.

### Tournament flow and inbox
- Return to Tournament Hub after confirming preparation. Advance there, then open Match Preview when ready to play.
- Add Skip Tournament to unentered invitation messages. Skipping simulates normal training, finances and CPU results up to seven days before the next eligible entry deadline.
- Pause that advance for required decisions, booked commitments, an existing entry or season review. Explain when the next entry window is already open or no eligible event remains.
- Keep entry, travel and preparation under the player's control. Entered events retain their separate withdrawal action.
- Explain the benefits, costs and training requirements of story responses, including cue-action rebuilding and coaching reviews.

### Sponsor temperament and contracts
- Give each company a stable Patient, Steady, Balanced, Reactive or Volatile temperament, with result reactions at 70%, 85%, 100%, 120% or 140% of the standard rate.
- More volatile sponsors lose satisfaction faster after defeats and recover faster after wins. Show the temperament before signing and in active contracts; preserve it through negotiation, renewal and reload.
- Make signing requirements, warning thresholds, recovery progress and walk-away conditions explicit in offers, contracts and sponsor emails.
- Keep a formal warning below 40 satisfaction, at least six further competitive matches before cancellation below 25, and warning clearance at 50. Promotional obligations remain separate.
- Older saves retain their satisfaction, warning progress and finances. Temperament affects future result reviews without replaying past matches.

### Windows download
Extract the entire Windows ZIP and launch **Snooker Career Manager.exe**. Keep all accompanying files together. The executable is unsigned.

Browser and desktop saves are separate. Export a browser career from Save Manager and import the JSON in the desktop game to transfer it.
