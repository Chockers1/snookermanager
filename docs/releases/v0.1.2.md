This release improves new-career onboarding, reduces routine inbox interruptions, corrects starting youth ability, and brings the career/storage work published since v0.1.1 into a numbered release.

### Onboarding and clearer tournament play
- The first-week guide lives in a dismissible popup and recognises ready equipment automatically.
- Its entry action opens the next eligible event in the correct calendar month and tour filter.
- New-career and tour-change briefings explain the pathway, event types and progression goals.
- Entry criteria explain age limits, mixed pathways and restricted invitational fields. Starting seeds are distinguished from earned rankings.
- Junior league fixtures are spread across event dates, with shared match-gap and hotel scheduling.
- Required career decisions block progression until answered. Project-conflict messages now let players keep their current project or cancel and start the proposed replacement directly, preserving earned attribute gains.

### Inbox and readability
- Entry, travel and preparation confirmations share one read receipt per event.
- Tour news updates one message per calendar week without repeatedly raising unread alerts.
- Detailed training reports are monthly, issued at the first training settlement in a new calendar month. Dates and accumulated changes are shown explicitly; a weekly report covering the same week is combined with it.
- Older fortnightly messages retain their original reporting periods. Training values and changes display two decimal places, including old inbox messages.
- Form evidence is readable on desktop, mobile and larger-text layouts; redundant preparation guidance is removed from Training.

### Player generation and career balance
- New careers assign pathway players' current ability using age, circuit and starting position. Potential is generated separately, so promising teenagers have room to develop instead of starting at elite professional strength.
- Existing saves retain their generated players' abilities and potential. Established professionals keep their starting-ability scale.
- Includes the previously committed cautious-opponent scoring fix, revised late-career physical decline, long-term sponsorship/economy changes, and explained career difficulty settings.

### Long-career storage
- Historical brackets and player seasons load on demand from durable archive chunks; current gameplay no longer needs every old bracket in memory.
- Named saves, recovery and portable export retain archived records and check missing or damaged data.
- Detailed earlier audits and their limitations are documented in the [career storage and balance report](https://github.com/Chockers1/snookermanager/blob/v0.1.2/docs/reports/career-storage-balance-2026-09-11.md). Those long simulations and benchmarks predate the final inbox and youth-generation changes; they are not a fresh full-career certification of every v0.1.2 change.

### Validation
- Full unit run: 855 tests exercised; 853 passed initially. Two test assumptions were corrected: ordinary season-news fixtures now specify their own ability/potential, and the tournament journey test accepts the Championship League's valid 2-2 draws. All 55 tests in the affected and related suites passed on rerun.
- 36 selected browser checks passed across desktop/mobile onboarding, event selection, training readability, inbox consolidation, project decisions, league dates, required-action gates, starting rankings, tour briefings, season news and report precision.
- Versioned production build, lint and staged whitespace checks passed.
- New-career rating generation was checked across youth, amateur, Q Tour and professional starts; older saved ratings remain unchanged. Monthly reports were checked across month/year boundaries, February, reloads and pending older report baselines.
