This release redesigns the management screens around clear tabs, compact summaries and accessible detail dialogs. It also integrates the supplied Snooker Career Manager branding and clarifies coaching and training effects.

### Management screens
- Redesigned Career Progression with a current-stage overview, connected full-pathway columns and a career snapshot showing match wins, tournament titles, major titles and World Championship titles.
- Reworked Legacy Stats with a prominent legacy and trophy summary, grouped records, larger trend charts and a dedicated Goals tab. Long histories remain available inside their tabs.
- Reworked Training, Staff, Equipment, Finance, Sponsorship, Rivalries and player overview layouts to use the available screen height, with long lists scrolling inside their panels.
- Added aligned sponsor-offer columns and Package, Comparison, Negotiation and Performance tabs for contract details. Contract actions stay visible.
- Replaced finance donuts with labelled income and expense bars.
- Added keyboard navigation for shared tabs and retained modal keyboard and focus behavior.

### Staff and training
- Staff slots now show coach ratings, strengths, weaknesses, specialisms and contract management together. Renewals and termination open from the team card.
- Displayed coaching benefits use the same specialism and training-gain calculation as actual development.
- Ending a coaching contract early pays out the remaining weeks at the agreed weekly rate. The cost is disclosed before confirmation, unaffordable termination is blocked, and the expense is recorded once.
- Contract extensions also extend the saved end date.
- Training locations now have authored practice strengths and a tradeoff: two attributes receive a 6% training benefit and one receives a 4% reduction while at the home base. These share the existing facility cap and do not represent claims about real national ability.
- Development-project and match-preparation values use display formatting that avoids floating-point tails while retaining simulation precision.

### Branding
- Integrated the supplied logos, title-menu and loading artwork, browser icons and web manifest.
- Preserved the full supplied asset pack, including marketing and store artwork. This is a web application release; no Steamworks upload or Windows installer is included.

### Validation
- All 927 unit tests passed across the full run and focused rerun. Four tests initially timed out under parallel load; all 28 tests in those four files passed with a single worker and unchanged timeouts.
- All 74 selected browser scenarios passed across the full run and focused rerun. One finance test was updated to open the new Season tab before checking opening cash.
- Browser coverage includes desktop, mobile and enlarged text, branding, archived saves, career decisions, player links, trophy filtering and reload, doubles reload, sponsor signing, staff contracts, finance, equipment and training.
- Production build, TypeScript, full lint and staged whitespace checks passed.
- No new multi-decade simulation was run for this management-interface release.
