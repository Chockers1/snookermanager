This release improves the Training, Legacy Stats and Settings workspaces and makes key match and recovery information easier to read without scrolling on desktop. It also fixes new-career difficulty selection and locks the chosen mode for the career.

### Training and career screens
- Redesigned the training timetable with clearer session cards, focus presets, saved/unsaved state and separate Plan, Development and Events summaries. Mobile uses a day selector.
- Preserved timetable editing, protected commitments, forecasts and the existing development, form and training-base editors.
- Redesigned Career Pathway event cards with dates, venues and live eligibility explanations, linking to the selected event in Calendar.
- Centred the Dashboard Career Details values and labels.

### Legacy Stats
- Updated Records, Trophies, Tournament History, Trends, Achievements and Stories while preserving the Overview layout.
- Renamed Goals to Achievements; existing Goals links still open the correct tab.
- Added distinct tournament-history collections and a career journal with story conclusions, interviews and seasonal summaries.
- Kept historical draws loading on demand, and fixed tournament selection returning to the wrong tab.
- Improved trend charts, exact-value summaries, trophy collections and achievement cards.

### Compact match and recovery views
- Reduced Match Preview's Matchup Analysis spacing while keeping all three approaches, explanations and comparison access.
- Compacted Mental and Health overview cards so assessment details, body risks, recovery figures and actions remain visible at tested desktop sizes, including enlarged text.

### Settings and difficulty
- Reorganised Settings into Display, Controls, Career and Support tabs with consistent layouts, keyboard access and bug-report export.
- Difficulty is selected during new-career setup and is read-only afterwards. Existing saves retain their stored mode; missing modes default to Standard.
- Fixed a setup bug that discarded Relaxed and Demanding selections when constructing a new career.
- Difficulty continues to affect background financial support and missed sponsor obligations only. Match rules, opponent abilities, training and prize money remain identical across modes.

### Validation
- All 930 unit tests across 89 files passed with two workers.
- All 44 release browser checks passed across desktop/mobile layouts, enlarged text, training edits and reload, historical archive loading, export/import, new-career difficulty selection, and recovery effects.
- Additional targeted checks passed for keyboard preferences, bug-report download and first-week guide reopening.
- TypeScript, production build and lint passed.
- No new multi-decade simulation was run for this interface and setup release.
