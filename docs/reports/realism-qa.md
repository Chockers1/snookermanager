## Regression coverage

- Live simulation pauses at intervals and overnight boundaries; choices survive reload and cannot repeat recovery.
- Old live saves receive sessions without retroactive fatigue rewards.
- One-click advancement traverses booked departure/arrival dates. Missing bookings stop the calendar before the departure window.
- Route origins follow previous events. Booked routes and prices stay fixed after arrival.
- Acclimatisation, elapsed base subscriptions and uncovered hotel nights settle once, including partial weeks.
- Familiarisation occupies time and affects conditions only from its date.
- Training access, affordability and subscription charges are exercised alongside the existing weekly pipeline.
- Scouting reviews use actual recorded matches, narrow uncertainty and do not change opponent attributes.
- Qualification uses scheduled expiries and ranking countback, preserves frozen positions and excludes similarly named youth/open events.
- Retired players are excluded from one-year rescue places.
- Digests use recorded upsets and rival scores, avoid duplicate news and do not promote qualifying-section winners to event champions.
