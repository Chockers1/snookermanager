# Rivalry visibility

Open Player > Rivalries from the sidebar. The page lists established rivalries and other recorded opponents, with search, filtering, head-to-head totals, recorded intensity, deciding frames, close matches, finals and up to eight surviving meetings. Retired players and practice partners are labelled.

A rivalry needs at least three meetings, including two deciders, three close matches or two finals. Intensity describes the relationship and is not the qualification threshold. Existing gameplay rules are unchanged.

Match preview, tournament hub and result screens now show recorded head-to-head history even before rivalry status. New rivalry announcements link to the dedicated page.

Old relationship totals are preserved. Missing intensity and detailed counters are shown as unknown. Surviving career match logs supply meeting details without replaying results or awarding statistics; ambiguous player names are excluded from that fallback.

Validation: 27 focused unit tests including existing career-depth coverage; desktop and mobile browser tests; production build and lint.
