# Seasonal sponsorship market

Sixty fictional companies form five sponsor levels: local, regional, national, international and global. At each season's commercial review, six companies approach the player with quoted monthly pay, contract length, brand fit, risk and a working event-win bonus. Offers reflect the player's current circuit, ranking and reputation. Youth and Q School number ones do not receive main-tour global pricing. Reaching a higher sponsor level creates two additional approaches once per level that season.

The annual cohort avoids companies from the previous season and existing active sponsors. Older unaccepted/rejected offers are replaced at rollover; active contracts keep their value, remaining duration and performance expectations. New-game markets contain only relevant seasonal approaches. Existing saves retain their current offers for the remainder of the season and receive a new cohort immediately on load.

Company selection and quotes are deterministic per world seed, season and brand. Quote values, negotiation counts and rejected status persist across recalculation and reload. Signing checks the current season and commercial requirements; signed offers cannot be reused after termination. Stale contract links show an unavailable page. A dated inbox announcement links to the offer screen, which displays the current market profile and seasonal labels.

Negotiations preserve agreed pay. Small contracts cannot gain money when reducing obligations or grow longer when requesting a shorter term. Fixed a pre-existing case mismatch in final/quarter-final/semi-final bonus checks. New contracts identify event bonuses by tournament and edition date so a multi-year deal can pay again next season; legacy contract bonus identities remain compatible.

Validation covers deterministic generation, circuit/reputation distinctions, 20 annual market rotations, active-contract preservation, promotion approaches, expiry, signing, negotiations, bonus payments and real season rollover. Desktop and phone browser checks exercise reload, negotiation, signing and stale links, plus short low-value contract negotiation. No live career save was edited.
