# Sponsor performance expectations

Active sponsors have a persisted 0–100 satisfaction score. New deals and old saves start at 75; historic matches are not replayed. Targets are fixed when the performance record is created: a 40–55% base match win target depending on career rank, increased by 5 for medium-risk and 10 for high-risk deals, plus room to slip approximately 25% in the current ranking table (at least four places).

Only newly settled competitive matches change satisfaction. Wins help, losses hurt modestly, draws receive half-win credit, tougher opponents soften expectations, and single-frame events have half weight. Missing the agreed ranking target adds a small penalty only on the same circuit. Each match changes satisfaction by at most five points. Exhibitions and idle weeks have no performance penalty.

Below 50 generates a concern message. Below 40 gives a formal warning and at least six further competitive matches to recover. Cancellation requires remaining below 25 after that recovery period. Reaching 50 clears the warning. Ending a deal removes its future income and frees the sponsor slot, with an inbox explanation. Already earned bonuses remain paid. Poor satisfaction also prevents renewal offers and acceptance of stale offers.

The active-deal cards show satisfaction, mood, latest change and warning progress; expandable details explain targets and recovery. Offer and contract screens disclose expectations before signing. Active deals remain visible even when no open offers remain. Existing promotional compliance duties remain separate and visible.

Validation covers mild isolated losses, sustained decline, the full warning period, recovery, duplicate settlement, exhibitions, idle weeks, circuit changes, stale renewals, actual match cancellation and income removal, plus desktop/mobile display and reload persistence.
